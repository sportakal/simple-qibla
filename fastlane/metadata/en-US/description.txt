Simplest Qibla Finder shows the Qibla direction the easiest way possible: on a real satellite map, with a clear line drawn from where you stand all the way to the Kaaba in Makkah.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the app — your location and the Qibla line appear instantly.
• Switch to compass mode and the map turns with your phone, so the line matches the real world around you.
• Follow the "Turn right / Turn left" hint. When you face the Qibla, the arrow turns green and you feel a gentle vibration.

SEE THE WHOLE WAY
Tap one button to zoom out and see the full route from your city to the Kaaba.

ACCURATE AND HONEST
• Uses true north and the great-circle direction, the standard method for finding the Qibla.
• Warns you when the compass is disturbed by metal or magnets, and tells you how to fix it.
• Lets you know if your location is approximate and whether it affects the direction.

APPLE WATCH
A simple dial on your wrist shows the Qibla at a glance — no need to take out your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used only on your device to calculate the direction.

Available in English, Turkish and Arabic.
